Ecommerce Catalogue

Overview

The Ecommerce Catalogue feature is an AI-powered creative tool designed to bring product marketing to life. By pairing dynamic AI influencer character models with static product images, users can generate high-quality, engaging video showcases for social media, digital campaigns, and eCommerce channels.

1. Generating a Product Showcase Video

To create a new product showcase showcase, navigate to the Ecommerce Catalogue tab and click Create Catalogue. The creation page provides settings to define your video's content, look, and style:

  • Influencer Model (Character): Pick a virtual AI character model from your generated personas library to act as the presenter.
  • Product Image: Select a static product photo from the Media Library to showcase.
  • Generative Scene Prompt: Write a descriptive text detail outlining how the character should showcase the product, including background environment, actions, lighting, and camera motions.
  • Aspect Ratio: Define the target resolution output. Choose standard aspect ratios like 9:16 (best for mobile reels, stories, or TikToks) or 16:9 (for desktop and YouTube).
  • Duration: Adjust the duration slider of the generated showcase video.
  • Sound & Watermark Toggles: Toggle whether the output includes audio, watermarks, or customized background music.

2. Prompt Engineering Helpers

To maximize output quality, the creator includes integrated helpers to optimize your generative prompts:

  • Improve Prompt: Enhance your scene description with AI, automatically adding detail, cinematography directions, and lighting keywords under 220 characters.
  • Add Details: Apply a random preset keyword (e.g., "cinematic product demo", "professional studio lighting", "smooth camera pan") to instantly enrich the prompt.
  • Surprise Me: Apply a completely pre-configured product marketing prompt for instant creative inspiration.
  • Prompt Library: Access pre-curated video marketing prompts in your prompt templates database.
3. Video Showcase Library

All generated videos are saved in the catalogue library. The library dashboard lets users browse their videos in a responsive grid, watch completed showcases in an inline video player, read the prompt used, review credit costs, download the files directly, or delete showcase items.

Key Points for Showcase Creators
  • Use clear, focused product images with simple backgrounds for optimal blending results.
  • Custom characters and generated models can be used repeatedly to maintain brand identity.
  • Sound options allow integration of background music URLs directly into the video file.
