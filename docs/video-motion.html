Video Motion

Overview

Video Motion is an AI-powered feature that allows users to create face swap and character replacement videos using uploaded images and reels. Users can upload a character image along with a video or reel, and the system intelligently replaces the original face in the video with the uploaded image while maintaining realistic motion, expressions, and animations.

This feature helps creators generate engaging AI reels, personalized videos, and dynamic character animations with smooth facial movement and realistic visual results.

1. Create Video Motion

In the Video Motion section, users can easily generate face swap videos using the provided interface. The process involves uploading a character image and selecting a video or reel to apply the face swap transformation.

  1. Upload a Reference Video to use an already added video or upload your own video as a motion reference.
  2. Upload a Reference Image to use an already added image or upload your own image for motion generation.
  3. Enter a Creative Prompt to describe the desired video motion, animation style, camera angles, camera movement, lighting, transitions, scene atmosphere, visual effects, and overall cinematic look of the generated video.
  4. Configure Motion Parameters such as Aspect Ratio, Resolution, and Sound settings to customize the final video output.
  5. Select Aspect Ratio: Choose the desired aspect ratio (e.g. 9:16, 1:1, 16:9) for the output video. This determines the dimensions and orientation of the generated video.
  6. Choose Motion Duration: Select the duration of the motion sequence (e.g., 5s, 10s, 15s). The system will generate motion up to the selected duration.
  7. Generate Motion Button: Clicking this button starts the video motion generation process. The system uses the uploaded reference video, reference image, and creative prompt to generate a new video with the specified motion.
  • Use AI-powered motion generation to transform images and videos into dynamic cinematic scenes with realistic movement and effects.
  • Manage Aspect Ratio, Resolution, and Sound Settings to customize the final video output according to your requirements.
  • Generate high-quality AI-powered cinematic motion videos with a single click.
