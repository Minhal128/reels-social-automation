Scheduled Posts

Overview

Scheduled Posts gives users a clear view of all content queued for future publishing. Posts created from the Composer or imported via Bulk Posts with a future date are collected here. This queue allows you to review pending content, adjust timing, or cancel scheduled items before they go live — all from a single management view.

1. Scheduled Queue Dashboard

The Scheduled Posts dashboard lists all pending posts in chronological order. Each card shows:

  • Scheduled Date & Time: The exact moment the post is set to publish.
  • Target Platforms: Icons representing each connected social channel the post is destined for.
  • Caption Preview: A snippet of the post text for quick identification.
  • Media Thumbnail: A preview of any attached image or video.
  • Status Badge: Current state — Pending (awaiting scheduled time) or Processing.

2. Managing Scheduled Posts

From the queue, each scheduled post card offers action controls:

  • Edit Post: Open the post in the Composer to modify the caption, media, or platform targets before it publishes.
  • Reschedule: Change the publish date and time directly from the queue without entering the full composer.
  • Cancel / Delete: Remove the post from the queue permanently. This does not delete the source media from the library.
