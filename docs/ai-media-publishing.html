Social Media Sharing & Publishing

Overview

The platform supports direct publishing to connected social media accounts such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, YouTube, Threads, and TikTok. Users can instantly share generated media, uploaded files, or admin-uploaded content without manually downloading or re-uploading files.

Supported publishing formats include:

  • Posts : Share AI-generated or uploaded images and videos directly to Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, and Threads feeds for regular audience engagement and content publishing.
  • Story : Publish temporary visual updates in story format, supported on Facebook and Instagram, ideal for promotions, announcements, quick highlights and interactive social engagement.
  • Reels / Shorts / Videos : Share short-form video content, AI-generated motion videos or animated clips as Reels (Facebook, Instagram, Threads) or YouTube/TikTok Videos/Shorts to increase reach and audience interaction.
Before sharing AI-generated media as Posts, Stories, Reels, or Videos directly to connected social accounts — social publishing is available only after platform connection.
  • Direct publishing to connected Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, YouTube, Threads, and TikTok accounts
  • Instant sharing without manual download or re-upload process
  • Reusable media assets for future campaigns and content creation
